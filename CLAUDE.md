# Wingcalc

Wing loading / cube loading / stall speed / stall character calculator for RC fixed-wing aircraft. Vue 3, plain JavaScript, Vite. See [README.md](README.md) for the overview, [docs/methods.md](docs/methods.md) for formulas and methods, [docs/user-guide.md](docs/user-guide.md) for the UI, and [SCOPE.md](SCOPE.md) for the original brief and build status.

## Conventions

- UK English everywhere (UI copy, comments, docs).
- All calculation code works in SI base units (m, m², kg, m/s); conversion happens only at the edges (`src/lib/units.js`, `UnitInput.vue`).
- Pure logic lives in `src/lib/` with no Vue imports, so it can be unit-tested directly.
- Styles live in one file, `src/styles/wingcalc.css`, scoped under `.wingcalc` / `wc-` class names. No `<style>` blocks in components.
- Aerofoil data is bundled at build time (`npm run fetch-airfoils`), never fetched from the internet in the browser. `airfoils.json` (polar-backed, eager) and `uiuc.json` (full UIUC catalogue, lazy chunk).
- XFOIL runs in the browser: pure parts (command script, parser, comparison) in `src/lib/xfoil.js`; the WebAssembly engine in `src/lib/xfoilEngine.js` (also used directly by the tests); the worker in `src/workers/`; queueing and the in-memory cache in `src/composables/usePolarSolver.js`; the stall-Re fixed point in `performanceWithXfoil` (`src/lib/airfoils.js`). Never call the engine on the main thread.
- Licence is GPL-3.0-or-later (XFOIL and WebXFOIL are GPL-2.0-or-later); keep `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` current when bundling anything new.
- Wing geometry for panel mode lives in `src/lib/wing.js` (flat s / projected y coordinates, Bézier edges); `calc.js` delegates to it.
- Public docs (`README.md`, `docs/user-guide.md`, `docs/methods.md`, `docs/aerofoil-data.md`) describe the app as it is: no open questions, caveats to ourselves or links to scope files. Those go in `docs/backlog.md`. Update the user guide and methods when a change alters behaviour or a formula.
- Decisions for later rounds are in `docs/scope-v2.md` and `docs/scope-v3.md`. The road to a whole aircraft is three bodies of work: `docs/scope-v8.md` (the wing, finished), `scope-v9.md` (the rest of the plane) and `scope-v10.md` (make it fly).
- Projects: `src/lib/projects.js` (storage injected, every access guarded). Saved state is `{ units, input, airfoil }`; anything added to `input` must also go in `defaultState()` so older saves migrate. `input.structure` and `input.exportOptions` are filled from their defaults recursively (`fillDefaults`), so new nested settings migrate too.
- Section editor ("Advanced" on a custom section): the geometry is pure in `src/lib/sectionSpline.js`; the drawing is `src/components/SectionEditor.vue`, the side panel and XFOIL preview `src/components/SectionSide.vue`, wired in `WingCalc.vue`. The result is `airfoil.custom.drawn` (saved with the project).
- Wing at any span station: `wingSampler` in `src/lib/wing.js` (chord, LE, twist, thickness, dihedral by flat s); `projectedSampler` looks it up by projected y for the lifting line.
- Aerodynamics: `src/lib/liftingLine.js` (Glauert series); `airfoilPerformance` in `airfoils.js` uses it for wing CLmax and stall onset in panel mode. Flaps and crow: `src/lib/flaps.js` (flight configurations, increments from GDES FLAP polars or flap theory) feeds `effects` into `wingStallAt`; flapped polar cache keys include the hinge and deflection (`flapKey`).
- Structure: `src/lib/structure.js` (materials, Stage A loads and margins, rib positions), `src/lib/ribs.js` (rib and part geometry, cut list, weight), `src/lib/dxf.js` (R12 DXF, kerf and dogbones, plan, CSV), `src/lib/strokeFont.js` (engraved labels, Hershey Sans 1-stroke from `src/data/strokeFont.json`, made by `npm run build-stroke-font`), `src/lib/wing3d.js` (3D geometry as plain arrays; `Structure3D.vue` loads three.js on demand and renders on demand, not in a loop). Part geometry is in metres in its own (u, z) plane; DXF converts to millimetres.
- Edge stock: `src/lib/edgeStock.js` (SLEC catalogue, fit check, auto pick). `analyseStructure` resolves it and returns the resolved `structure`; pass `a.structure`, not `input.structure`, to `buildParts`, the plan, 3D and export.
- Foam-core wings: `structure.construction` 'foam' with settings in `structure.foam` (`src/lib/foam.js`: materials, defaults, wrinkling and core shear). `analyseStructure` lays a foam wing out as its equivalent fully sheeted, rib-less structure (`foamStructure`), so `a.structure` is that (construction kept), `a.ribs` is empty and `a.cores` lists the panels; templates, skins, webs and pocket templates come from `src/lib/foamParts.js` via `buildParts`.
- Pieces in the 3D view: each solid `wing3d` adds carries a `piece` ({ id, name, material }) and, when it's cut as a part, a `pick` (ids, a gusset member, or groups plus a surface, resolved by `partFor`). `wingPieces(model, parts)` lists every piece with its part (null for strip stock, servos, cores); the 3D view selects pieces and the Part card shows one as placed. A new solid needs a piece, and a new part drawn in 3D a pick; `tests/partPick.test.js` checks both. The model is built once in WingCalc (`model3d`) for the 3D view and the Part card.
- View controls (wing editor, structure plan, 3D): no toolbar above a drawing. − + Fit in the bottom-right corner (`StageZoom.vue`, with a Snap toggle in views that edit; F, + and − while the view has focus); no tool rail or modes where they can be avoided (double-click adds a rib; Measure is the one mode, a toggle); view toggles top left (Measure; the 3D view's Layers and Section); actions in a command bar along the top (`wc-cmd`), on the selection or, with nothing selected, on the drawing (Add rib, Add panel); the 3D section bar along the bottom while a cut is on. Fit keeps the drawing clear of these. Undo/Redo are window-level (`UndoRedo.vue` in the page and editor headers; Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y handled in WingCalc). Icon path data lives in `src/lib/icons.js`.
- Logo: `src/lib/logo.js` draws the mark, a sailplane from above in white on the blue tile (`iconSvg`), used for the favicon, the app icons and the header. `npm run build-icons` writes `public/` (favicon.svg/.ico, apple-touch-icon, manifest icons) and the README logo (`docs/images/logo-light.png`, `logo-dark.png`, the wordmark set from the bundled font); the files are committed. The wordmark is IBM Plex Sans, bundled.
- No native browser dialogues (`alert`, `confirm`, `prompt`). Ask with `ask({ title, message, confirm, danger })` in WingCalc, which shows `ConfirmDialog.vue` and resolves true to go ahead; report outcomes in the project bar's status line.
- Unit quantities include `thin` (lengths shown to 0.1 mm, for material sizes) and `percent` (a fraction shown as %).
- Inputs commit on Enter or blur, never per keystroke (`UnitInput.vue`); inspector sections are an accordion (one open per parent element, `InspectorSection.vue`).
- Warnings sit beside the settings that fix them: main-page ones under their input (`warn` as a message), structure ones in the editor section mapped by id in `src/lib/warnings.js`. Give new structure warnings an id that maps there.
- Control surfaces: where a surface is (extent, depth, throws, crow) is the wing editor's (`ControlSurfaceEditor.vue`, handles in `WingPlanEditor.vue`); its hinge gap and servo bay are the structure editor's (`SurfaceStructure.vue`). A surface hinged at the TE stock's face is the stock itself (`stock` in `surfaceLayouts`).
- Servos: generic classes and Hitec models in `src/lib/servos.js`; a bay's servo goes through `installedServo(id, mounting)`. Trays sit between the wing's own ribs where they fit (`fitBaysToRibs`).
- D-box sheeting per surface and sheeted hard points: `sheetExtent` and `hardPatches` in `structure.js`; use them rather than testing `covering.type` for where sheeting runs.
- The drawn section's trailing edge is always closed (`closeTrailingEdge` / `syncTrailingEdge` in `sectionSpline.js`).

## Testing

- Runner: **Vitest** (`npm test`). Chosen because it shares Vite's config and transform pipeline, so tests import the same modules (including the bundled JSON) with no extra setup.
- Tests live in `tests/` and cover `src/lib/` and `src/composables/`. `tests/xfoilEngine.test.js` runs real XFOIL polars (a few seconds). Components are not unit-tested; check them in the running app. `tests/render.test.js` renders the whole app on the server from a saved state and fails on any error or Vue warning: a quick crash check, not a substitute for the running app.
- The browser pane may be hidden, in which case the page gets no animation frames and ResizeObserver never fires. Don't mistake that for a bug, but don't rely on either for first paint.

## Data fetch etiquette

airfoiltools.com returns 403 if hit too quickly. The fetch script runs one request at a time with a 1.5 s gap, backs off on 403/429, and caches every response in `scripts/.cache/` (gitignored). Don't lower the delay; re-runs come from the cache.
