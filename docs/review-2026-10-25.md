## Servo: decisions to make

- [x] **Hatch for a top-side servo.** A horn on top puts the servo under the upper skin, and it goes into its plate from above, but the hatch and rails are still in the lower skin. Should the hatch move to the upper skin?

- [x] **Horn shape.** The horn's outline is a placeholder: its base on the surface behind the bevel, 70% of its height long, and a column 2.5 mm either side of the hinge for its holes. Which real horn shapes do we model?

Placeholders are fine for now.

- [x] **Screw blocks.** These are spruce, 6 mm along the span, as long as the lug beyond the case, and 3 mm past the screw. Are the size and material right? On a servo with short lugs they may be too short to glue well.

Also fine. They'll be invented by the builder, not pre-cut.
- [x] **Minimum ply between notches** is 4 mm (`MIN_PLY`). Is that right for 1.5 mm ply?

- [x] **Fore-aft position** is limited to between the lead strip behind the spar and the hinge spar, as the automatic position is. Should it be allowed further forward where the wing allows?

## Servo: changed behaviour for older projects

- A bay that relied on the old default horn on top now has its horn underneath, and its servo on the lower skin.
- A bay saved flat, upright or turned a quarter loads on its side, unturned.
- The bevel swap changes the hinge spars and trailing-edge stock of every project with control surfaces.
- Bays are wider, so a bay that used to sit between the wing's own ribs may now get its own pair.

## Structure: assumptions made

- [x] **Cap strips (2.1):** the D-box problem was an old saved 10% cap-strip start, which the D-box editor hides. That setting now applies only to an open structure.
- [x] **Strength check (2.2):** it assumes the shallower "keep 4 mm" spar everywhere unless every thin rib is split. That's on the safe side, because the check runs before the ribs are placed.
- [x] **Thin-rib choices (2.2):** each choice is saved against the rib's name (R12), as brace thicknesses are, so it moves to a different rib if ribs are added or removed.
- [x] **Second web (2.4):** Box is now the "second web" checkbox; older saves keep `box` internally.

Rather than second web, give options of front/back/both webs

- [x] **Inside rod or tube (2.5):** it goes through a hole with 0.1 mm clearance; on a skin it sits in a square notch, not a round-bottomed one.

If this is talking about stringers and reinforcement, the notch inner edge should match the profile of the reinforcement, so the stock can be pushed in from the edge.

- [x] **Servo bay ribs (7 November):** a tray sits between the wing's own ribs only when they're no more than 1.5× the servo's bay apart (`TRAY_SLACK`); further than that, the bay gets its own pair of ribs. Item 1 of the notes was read as "the wing's ribs are too far apart to hold the servo".
- [x] **Shared lead hole (7 November):** the biggest plug's hole plus 1.5 mm per extra lead (`LEAD_EXTRA`), taking flat leads as about 1 mm thick.
- [x] **Leads through a lightening hole (7 November):** only a hole behind the spar counts, since that's where the leads run.
- [x] **Loads zoom (7 November):** along the span only; the margin axis keeps its fixed −1 to +3.

See words previously.
