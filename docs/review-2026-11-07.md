## Open: thin ribs, per-rib choices and where the caps stop

To be talked through with Matt before anything is built.

**Per-rib Split or Thin choices.** Each rib too thin for the spar has its own choice, saved against the rib's name, so adding a rib moves the choice to a different rib; and caps and solid spars share the saved choices with different meanings. Neither fix offered (save by distance, or clear on change) was accepted.

**Where the caps stop.** Confirmed by Matt on 14 November ("if the rib is too thin for the cap, do not add a cap until the rib is thick enough to thin for it"): working in from the tip, the spar caps start at the first rib deep enough to take a cap (thinned if need be) with the rib's margin left between them. Outboard of that there are no caps at all: the ribs are whole, and the strength check treats that stretch as having no spar, as it does beyond a short tube. That would replace today's behaviour, where caps always run to the tip and are thinned or the rib is split, and it would make the "cap thickness needed" figure's flaw moot.

To settle:

- Is "no cap" a third per-rib choice beside Split and Thin, or a rule that applies by itself wherever a thinned cap would be under some minimum thickness (and what minimum)?
- What carries the load outboard of the caps: sheeting only, or a stringer or reinforcement run on?
- Does the same rule apply to a solid spar that tapers to nothing? (A dihedral brace already isn't built where a solid spar is under 1 mm deep at the break, and says so.)
- With caps stopping short, do the per-rib choices still need saving at all, or does one wing-wide rule cover it?

## Questions on what was built

**Hinges.** SLEC's and Kavan's parts are in the Tabs list (SLEC's Mylar flat hinge and flocked cyano hinge; Kavan's small, standard and heavy duty nylon pin hinges), and Robart's hinge points under Pinned.

- None of the pages gives a thickness, so each has a typical one (0.25 and 0.3 mm for the Mylar ones; 1.0, 1.2 and 1.5 mm for the nylon ones), marked estimated. A calliper on one of each would settle them.
- The pages give each size as two figures without saying which way round. The longer is taken as across the hinge (26 × 19 mm means 13 mm into each spar). Say if that's wrong for any of them.
- Robart's hinge points (306, 308, 309: 3/32, 1/8 and 3/16 in): retailers give the shank diameter and the packet but no length, so the lengths (19, 28 and 38 mm) are typical figures marked estimated. A ruler on one of each would settle them.
- A nylon pin hinge's knuckle sits in the gap. The check is crude: it warns when the gap is under twice the leaf's thickness. Should the spars be notched for the knuckle instead, and drawn so?

**Servo hatch.** "Hatch made from" is built with a new piece of the skin's sheet as the default. Two of the three questions weren't answered and are assumed:

- No rebate or frame besides the two rails, and a new piece is the opening's exact size (not oversize).
- On a foam wing the piece is treated as a removable hatch on rails, the same as a built-up wing, not as a patch glued back flush.

### 14 and 15 November

Each checked in the app by Matt.

- **Shear.** Along a dihedral brace's arms the braces share the shear web's shear (or a solid spar's) by stiffness. Solid and tube spars are checked in shear ("Spar in shear" on the loads chart, with a warning). A web left at no thickness no longer reads as 0/0 over a brace.
- **Dihedral braces.** The 10 mm minimum is gone: a brace fills the depth inside the wing, never less than the spar, and is refused only where a solid spar is under 1 mm deep at the break. On a solid spar each brace has a height of its own, held between the spar and the depth inside the wing. A rib crossing a short brace is notched round the spar and plates where the rib margin allows, otherwise cut in two.
- **Custom trailing edge stock** has a thickness (blank: the thinnest that fits), and is checked and drawn like the catalogue sizes.
- **Turned to the camber** always turns the stock when it fits that way, to the camber's angle as near as it fits, catalogue stock included.
- **Trace image** in the section editor: a picture cropped, turned and placed on the chord by its LE and TE, behind the grid, kept while the app is open but not saved (`docs/scope-trace-image.md`).
