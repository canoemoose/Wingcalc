# Codebase review, 7 November 2026

A whole-codebase pass for consistency, silly bugs, and settings that act while hidden or do nothing while shown. Eight reviewers read every file in `src/`; the clear-cut findings were fixed in seven commits (`c95f6bd` to `93bd4df`, 763 tests green, build green, the app loaded and clicked through without console errors). This file holds what wasn't fixed: judgement calls to decide, and the things that changed behaviour or want a look by eye. Tick, decide, or move to `docs/backlog.md`.

## Behaviour that changed (worth knowing when an old project loads)

- **Hinge range.** One range, 5% to 99% of chord, in every path (depth mode, plan drags, typed values). A saved hinge under 5% now resolves as a problem (it used to pass above 2%). The 95% cap that stopped a stock-depth surface being the stock on chords over about 0.37 m is gone.
- **Plan depth readout** is measured from the surface's leading edge (half the gap behind the hinge line), so a 15 mm surface reads 15.0 mm, not 15.5.
- **Hitec torques** follow the same nearest-6 V rule as Futaba's: HS-7115TH 3.9 → 3.2, D89MW 8.5 → 5.3, D145SW 6.0 → 3.9 kg·cm.
- **Older saves migrate:** a box spar becomes caps with both webs; a retired flat leading-edge code becomes its symmetrical stock (the picker used to show Auto for it).
- **Strength check:** the cap-buckling rib-spacing limit now applies on a D-box whose compressed side has no sheeting over the spar (top side unticked, or `topTo` ahead of the spar), not only on an open wing. "From loads" spacing can come out closer on such wings. The tissue rib-spacing check now covers tapered spacing too.
- **Hidden settings no longer act:** a hard point's D-box sheet thickness counts only on a D-box; cap strips only when the covering isn't fully sheeted (they used to widen servo bays and change the turned leading edge on a sheeted wing).
- **Undo** records the mass, the size in Mean chord and Area modes and the mode itself; a change still inside the 400 ms debounce is undone as its own step; a drag is one step however long it pauses; Ctrl+Z works after a checkbox or slider and is off behind dialogues.
- **XFOIL** no longer dies after about 45 polars in one session (a wasm stack leak in WebXFOIL; `docs/aerofoil-data.md` has the mechanism).

## Decisions to make

- [ ] **Servo hatch counted twice.** The hatch is a separate part (SB1-H, laid out on fresh stock, weighed) *and* the sheet it says it's cut from has no opening, so the weight counts the area twice and the DXF cuts the hatch again. Cut the opening from the sheet and keep the hatch as its own part, or treat it as cut from the sheet (no separate layout, no extra weight)?
- [ ] **Strength check and hard-point sheets.** (a) The bending section counts a hard point's sheet right back to the trailing-edge stock, but the sheet as cut stops at the fixed hinge spar (small optimism, shallow depth there). (b) The sheet adds to bending but isn't itself checked for crushing or buckling: not at all on an open wing, only ahead of the spar on a D-box, and at the wing's thickness rather than its own on a sheeted wing. Check the patch strips at their own thickness wherever they're counted?
- [ ] **Thinned caps and "cap thickness needed".** Where caps are thinned to keep the rib margin, the readout multiplies the *nominal* thickness by a ratio from the *thinned* cap's stress, so it overstates. Use the thinned thickness, and suggest splitting the rib instead where thicker caps can't help?
- [ ] **Spar fit check assumes sheeting over the spar.** `sparSection` tests the skin without a side, so for a D-box with `topTo` short of the spar the "doesn't fit" check still allows for sheeting there. Check top and bottom separately?
- [ ] **Per-rib Split/Thin choices are keyed by rib name** (R12), so adding a rib moves a saved Split to the next rib; caps and solid spars share the map with different meanings ("margin" is Thin caps for one, Taper spar for the other). Key by distance, or clear the choices when the spar type changes?
- [ ] **Foam wing's spar position** is hidden with the Spar section but still places hinges, servo bays and air brakes, and a surface too far forward gets "would run into the main spar". Show a "reference line" position for foam, with foam wording?
- [ ] **Sheeted hard point on an open wing** is cut from the sheeting material, whose picker is hidden on an open wing. Give the hard point its own material picker?
- [ ] **Cap strip material** (`capStripMaterial`) is in the cut list and 3D but no control sets it. Add a picker beside width and thickness, or hard-code it?
- [ ] **Section editor has no Snap toggle**, though CLAUDE.md asks for one in views that edit. Add grid snapping, or record the exemption?
- [ ] **Configurator warnings name controls it doesn't have** (horn position, horn turn, square to hinge, bay centre, all in the surface's side panel). Say where they are, or bring them into the dialogue?
- [ ] **Command bar placement.** On the section editor the centred bar probably overlaps the top-left toggles at stage widths around 1000 to 1100 px, and at phone width Fit doesn't allow for the bar moving to the bottom. Needs a look by eye.
- [ ] **Rib labels** follow different size rules from every other part (1.5 to 5 mm vs 2 to 4 mm), the fallback label isn't checked against the outline, and the leading-edge rod notch isn't in the keep-clear set.
- [ ] **`le-round` recommends "the largest rod that fits"** found at the panel ends only, while the fit is re-checked at every rib.
- [ ] **Air brake depth ignores D-box sheeting** ahead of the spar (`brakeLayouts.depthAt` passes a rear face of 0). Brakes are due a rethink anyway (backlog).
- [ ] **User guide's foam section** says dihedral braces are set as on a built-up wing, but the Braces section is hidden on foam wings. Which is right?
- [ ] **"Leading edge stock, height at the rib face"** still says rib on a foam wing (one word).
